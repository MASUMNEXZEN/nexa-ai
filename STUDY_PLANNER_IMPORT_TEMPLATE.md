## Publishing rules

- The admin must confirm ownership or authorization.
- The source must be owner-verified or reviewer-verified.
- Preview must show zero invalid records.
- Correct answer keys must match an option.
- English academic content is required for student serving.
- Missing figures, incomplete questions, or Bengali-only questions remain unpublished until corrected.
